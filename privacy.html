Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information may be handled when you use the NexaCart website and how that information is treated.

NexaCart is currently presented as a front-end e-commerce portfolio experience. Some features shown on the website are demonstrations and are not connected to a live payment or order-processing system.

1. Introduction

Welcome to NexaCart. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information may be handled when you interact with this website.

By using the website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how the website is used, information may include details that you voluntarily enter into forms, such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping information entered during checkout
  • Messages or feedback submitted through the contact form

NexaCart does not intentionally request sensitive personal information through the standard website experience.

3. How We Use Information

Information provided through the website may be used to demonstrate or support features such as customer communication, checkout forms, order summaries, and website functionality.

Information should only be provided when it is necessary for the feature you are using.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

NexaCart may use browser technologies such as local storage to make the front-end shopping experience work.

For example, the shopping cart may be temporarily stored in your browser so that products remain available while you navigate between pages.

This browser-stored information is part of the website's front-end functionality and should not be considered a secure database for production customer information.

5. Information Sharing

NexaCart does not represent that information entered into this portfolio demonstration is automatically transmitted to a live store operator or third-party service.

If the website is later connected to payment providers, analytics services, email platforms, hosting services, or other third-party systems, the privacy policy should be updated to accurately describe those integrations.

6. Security

We aim to use reasonable practices when developing and presenting the website. However, a static front-end website should not be treated as a secure environment for storing sensitive customer information.

A production version of NexaCart should use appropriate server-side security, authentication, encrypted connections, secure databases, and trusted payment infrastructure.

7. Your Choices

You can choose whether to provide information through optional website forms.

You can also clear browser storage through your browser settings. Doing so may remove locally stored cart or other front-end website data.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website's features, services, integrations, or legal requirements change.

The latest version will be displayed on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact NexaCart through the Contact page.
